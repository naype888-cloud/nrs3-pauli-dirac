Dirac, flat and curved

Time as a fourth generator forces 4 × 4 (1928). In 1929 Weyl and Fock–Ivanenko bent it: a tetrad e writes the metric as g = eᵀηe and the matrices become Γμ = eaμγa. Space, time and gravity in one line:

(Γ(p) − m)(Γ(p) + m) = (g(p, p) − m²) · 1
